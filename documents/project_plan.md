# WP7 plan
This workpackage is responsible for the design and implementations of demonstrators. 
These demonstrators are showcases demonstrating the abilities of NAIC. The establishment of a demonstrator requires

the connection to a community (either academia, industry, or public sector),
the development of a use case of relevance for the community and its formulation as a ML/AI task, and
a ML/AI pipeline (software) to solve the task.

The demonstrators are developed under the lead on NORCE and in collaboration with Sigma2, Simula, Sintef, UiA, and UiB. 
The partners will make use of the established networks in to find community partners and with their expertise on 
different areas. The development of the use cases will be handled case by case.
Establishing a use case has often a part of explaining ML/AI to users. This is true for all the sectors 
(academia, public administration, and industry). This is often also necessary to convince a user of the value 
which lies in ML/AI, and be active or take the lead in formulating the use case. In only some cases users do 
have sufficient knowledge in ML/AI.
Technically use cases are complete pipelines 'data in ... processing ... results out'. They have a 
data science/ML/AI methodology which is used, and compute facility in the back. 
They will be realized as python notebooks with associated python environments. They will be clearly 
structured, easy to read, and easy to understand. These notebooks will be made available to the public 
as software (git-) repositories, as interactive notebooks running on Sigma2's resources, and as 
documents (pdf). Video tutorials will be created to demonstrate the possibilities.
It shall be noted that only in cases where users wish to have a complete solution 
(problem → technical software solution), and where users agrees to have a public demonstration 
of the solution, the case will give rise to a demonstrator. In other cases, NAIC can still be of 
assistance (training, consulting), but a demonstrator will not be realized. These cases will not be handled by this WP.
